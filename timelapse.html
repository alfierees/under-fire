The Spread

Every month since January 2020, drawn on the map. For years the fire was a Gaza-border story — one red corner of an otherwise dark map. Then October 2023, and front after front lights up until, by the 2026 war, all 30 alert regions are burning at once. Press play, or drag the timeline.

What the animation shows

From one front to every front

Before October 2023, only one month in nearly four years exceeded a thousand alerts nationwide — May 2021, the Guardian of the Walls war, with 6,331. Twenty-three of Israel's 30 alert regions had ever been hit. After October 7 the map changes state: Hezbollah lights up the Confrontation Line for fourteen straight months, Houthi fire reaches Eilat and the centre, Iran's direct barrages flash nationwide in April and October 2024 and June 2025 — and the war of February 2026 becomes the biggest month on record, 58,608 alerts in March 2026 alone, with every single region under fire.
