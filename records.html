October 7, 2023 — as it happened

October 7, 2023 is no longer the busiest day in the record — the 2026 war has passed it — but it remains the day the war began, minute by minute. Each bar is one minute between 06:29 and 23:13. The early-morning spike runs for roughly three hours before the cadence settles into a steady, exhausted drumbeat.
