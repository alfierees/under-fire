Structuring six years of alerts over Israel.

Between 2020 and 2026, Israel was the target of an unprecedented sustained campaign of rocket, missile, and drone attacks from Hamas in Gaza, Hezbollah in Lebanon, the Houthis in Yemen, and the Islamic Republic of Iran. Under Fire is an attempt to structure, visualise, and preserve that record, using frontier AI, open-source intelligence, and human editorial oversight.
